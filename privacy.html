Updated October 12, 2026

This policy covers the Antizionism Detector application and related research tools published by Kile B. Jones.

What the tool is

The Antizionism Detector analyzes language, not people. It never identifies, names, or reports any individual. It makes no judgment about any person — it flags patterns in text. It is an educational and research tool.

What is collected

No personal information is collected or stored — no names, no email addresses, no account details, no location, and no browsing history.

Only if you agree to the on-screen consent notice, the text you submit and its result are saved privately for research into patterns of language. These records are never used against anyone and exist only to study how this language works. Please do not include personal or confidential information in text you submit.

How research data is handled

Saved submissions are used solely for Kile Jones's research into antizionist language. They are not sold and are not shared with advertisers. If you decline the consent notice, nothing is saved. These standards are the same as those of any research survey.

Your choices

To request deletion of a submission, contact the email below. Declining consent saves nothing. Clearing your browser data removes any local history kept on your device.
