Kile B. JonesKile B. Jones

Kile Jones researches and writes on modern antizionism, history, and the uses of language.

He holds two master’s degrees in Religious Studies from Boston University, founded and edited the Claremont Journal of Religion at the Claremont Colleges, and serves as Director of Design and Creative Strategy at Stop Antizionism.

His work on antizionism has appeared in Fathom Journal and The Times of Israel. His earlier writing on philosophy and religion appeared in Philosophy Now, The Humanist, The Routledge Guide, Zygon, and Free Inquiry.
